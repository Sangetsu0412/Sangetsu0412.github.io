Jarvis privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Who uses Jarvis

Jarvis is a personal assistant used only by its owner on their own computer. It has no other users.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's consent, Jarvis requests read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly), Google Calendar (calendar.readonly), and the owner's basic profile and email address. It cannot send, delete or modify email, and cannot create or change calendar events.

How the data is used

Email and calendar content is read only when the owner asks a question, to answer that question. The sign-in token is stored on the owner's computer. Content needed to answer a question is sent to the AI model provider (Anthropic) that powers the assistant, under the owner's own account.

Sharing and storage

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else. Jarvis does not keep a copy of email or calendar content beyond answering the question asked.

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
